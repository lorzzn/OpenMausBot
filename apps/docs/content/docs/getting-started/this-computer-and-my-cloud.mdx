---
title: This computer and My Cloud
description: What lives where when you have both, and how to bring things from one to the other.
---

With MausBot Cloud you have two places in one app: **This computer**, the
OpenMausBot that runs on your Mac or PC, and **My Cloud**, the one that keeps
running when your computer is off. You switch between them with the server
switcher at the top of the sidebar.

## What lives where

Each place keeps its own **bots, chats, skills, routines, memory and logins**.
Nothing syncs by itself, so the two can look different: My Cloud may have your
Chief of Staff and nine other bots while this computer has only one. The
switcher says which side has which in one line, for example *"Your bots live on
My Cloud. This computer has its own: Ember."*

Two things are deliberately one per person rather than one per place: your
MausBot Cloud plan, and the computer you lend to My Cloud (see below).

## Making both sides match

All of these are one-way, from this computer to My Cloud, and you start each one:

- **Bots, chats, skills, routines and settings:** in **Settings → MausBot Cloud**,
  **Copy this computer's bots and chats** copies everything over the first time.
  Later, **Bring newer chats** adds the conversations My Cloud doesn't have yet,
  without replacing anything there. The switcher's **Bring this computer's bots
  and chats to My Cloud…** opens the same place.
- **Logins and 1Password:** in **Settings → Logins** on this computer, each login
  and the 1Password connection say whether they are **On this computer only** or
  **On this computer and My Cloud**. **Also use on My Cloud** sends that one login,
  or the 1Password service account, straight to My Cloud over the same
  connection Copy uses. It never goes through a chat, a bot or a log. A login
  kept to some bots stays kept to those of them My Cloud has: check **Who can use
  it** there afterwards.
- **Engine sign-ins and API keys** stay where you made them. Sign in to Claude,
  ChatGPT or another engine on My Cloud itself; its first chat shows how.

Going the other way, from My Cloud to this computer, is by hand: set it up again
in this computer's own Settings.

## Letting My Cloud use this computer

**Settings → MausBot Cloud → Let My Cloud use this computer** lets bots on My
Cloud use the folders and the apps and screen you choose here, while this
computer is awake with the app open. Turn it off there at any time, or from the
app's icon in the menu bar or taskbar.
